How is this calculated?

Current attendance = Present ÷ (Present + Absent) × 100. The full course hours are never used for this.

Minimum present = Full course × Required%. Maximum absence = Full course − Minimum present. Safe leave = Maximum absence − Absent so far.

Recovery (when below the requirement): smallest whole A with (P + A) ÷ (T + A) ≥ R, where T = completed hours.

Projection = (Present + Future hours) ÷ Full course × 100.
